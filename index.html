Fabrizzio Sabelli

I am a Ph.D. student in Applied Mathematics at Université de Montréal , supervised by Lucas Benigni. Before my Ph.D, I completed my Master's degree in Applied Mathematics under the supervision of Dr. Maciej Augustyniak at Université de Montréal. Prior to that, I completed my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Computer Science at McGill University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of random matrix theory, high-dimensional probability, optimization and machine learning.
